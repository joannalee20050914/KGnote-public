## Ordered gates

The order is exact-candidate deterministic verification, independent Ubuntu
Claude review, normal GitHub integration, immutable Mac/Ubuntu deployment,
then positive and negative no-model host acceptance. Deployment must read back
the exact integrated revision, tree, manifest and installed-file hashes.

No-model acceptance uses a separate PAUSED test identity. It must prove zero
formal reservations, attempts, checkpoints and provider processes, and must
re-read the prior state and ledger at their original SHA-256 values. It cannot
claim product development, human acceptance or release verification.

## Forbidden actions

- Do not publish `APPROVE`, `RESUME` or `ACCEPT`.
- Do not launch an O2 Codex or Claude product-model lane.
- Do not modify, truncate, replace, clear or recalculate the prior formal
  state, ledger or checkpoint history.
- Do not increase a provider ceiling or reset carried usage.
- Do not change the product outcome, Work Items, obligations, acceptance
  conditions or safety policy.
- Do not use a paid API, bypass GitHub rules, force push, direct-write main or
  promote a human/release gate.

If all deployment or activation prerequisites cannot be completed before the
proposed start, the Runner remains PAUSED and the authorization package must
report that a new date and immutable version are required. It must not silently
change this candidate schedule.

## Completion boundary

This execution Goal is complete only when the exact candidate is deployed on
both hosts, isolated no-model positive and negative acceptance has passed, the
prior formal history hashes are unchanged, and an auditable package is ready
for the Product Owner to decide whether to publish the exact `APPROVE` command.
Completion of this infrastructure Goal is not completion or activation of the
O2 Product Goal.
